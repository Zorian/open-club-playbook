01
The Big Idea

A media channel,
not a billboard

Most apps shout "download me" into the void and wonder why nobody listens. We do the opposite.

Open Club becomes the page golfers actually want to follow — the home of every clip, meme, interview, fact and bit of news worth seeing. We become the channel people check daily. Once we own the attention, the app is the obvious next step, sitting right there in the bio.

"We don't sell the app. We build the room everyone wants to be in — then we tell them where the door is."

That means the brand has to feel like a movement, not a product. Entertaining first, useful second, promotional last. If a post only exists to push downloads, it doesn't go up. Every post earns its place by being something a golfer would share with their mates.

02
How It Works

Instagram is HQ.
Two lanes.

Instagram is the head of the snake — our channel, our community, where everything starts. Inside Instagram we run two distinct content lanes, each with a different job.

Lane A · The Reach Engine

Reels

The job: get found. This is how new golfers discover us. High volume, high energy. We surface the best golf clips out there — trick shots, fails, funny moments, tour drama — and make them ours: our caption, our framing, our voice stamped on top. Most people will find Open Club through a reel before they ever see the feed.

Lane B · The Brand

Feed Posts

The job: look like a brand. Our own designed style. We take a clip, a stat or a story, and rebuild it on-theme with graphic design — clean layouts, our colours, and the red-circle markup highlighting the key moment. The feed is the shop window: it's what makes a first-time visitor hit follow.

● The signature look

The red circle is our visual signature — drawn around the moment that matters in a clip or graphic. It's instantly recognisable, it directs the eye, and it makes any repurposed content unmistakably Open Club. Use it consistently. It becomes the thing people associate with us.

03
The Content Engine

Make it once.
Post it everywhere.

We never create separately for each platform — that's how you burn out in a month. Everything is made for Instagram first, then flows down the same waterfall to every other channel.

IG
Step 01 · Source of truth

Instagram

Every piece is built here first. Reels go to the Reels tab, designed graphics go to the main feed. This is where the content is born and where the community lives.

TT
Step 02 · Repost, reformatted

TikTok

Every reel → reposted as a TikTok video. Every feed graphic / multi-image post → reposted as a TikTok photo slideshow. Same content, native format. Zero new creation.

YT
Step 03 · Reach overflow

YouTube Shorts

Every reel also goes up as a Short. Free extra reach on a platform desperate for short-form, off content we've already made.

● Rule of thumb

One unit of content = three posts across three platforms. The work happens once, on Instagram. Everything after that is distribution, not creation.

04
Posting Cadence

Twice a day.
Every day.

Consistency beats perfection. The target is two posts per day, per platform — a mix of reel and feed so we're feeding both the discovery engine and the brand window daily.

06
The Growth Lever

Collabs are
the cheat code

The single fastest way to grow is the collab post.

When we co-post with another golf page, our content lands in their followers' feeds too — instant exposure to an audience that already loves golf. We want as many collab posts as humanly possible with golf creators, media pages, clubs and personalities. Every collab is borrowed reach we didn't have to earn from scratch.

"One viral collab with the right golf page can do what three months of solo posting can't."

The play: build the page to a level worth collaborating with, then go out and partner with everyone in the golf space who'll say yes — and repost/credit them generously so they want to work with us again.

07
The Content Pipeline

A steady supply
of raw material

The content manager won't be hunting alone. A constant stream of golf material — latest news, updates, interesting facts, trending clips and angles — gets fed in to be rebuilt on-brand for the page.

The job at the desk: take that raw material and recreate it as Open Club content — reframed, redesigned, red-circled, and ready to post. We're not just reposting; we're rebuilding everything in our own style so it belongs to us.

● Repost the right way

When we resurface someone else's clip, we credit the original creator (tag + @ in caption), and we add value — our caption, our edit, our markup. This keeps us on the right side of creators (they become collab targets, not enemies), protects the page, and makes every repost feel additive, not lazy.
